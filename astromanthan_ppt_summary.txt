Explain more about problem statement

Problem Statement:
"PLANETARY FORGE: The Celestial Environment Prediction Challenge"

Key Challenge Points:
• The Complex Planetary Environment:
  A celestial body's environmental condition is governed by an intricate web of 
  physical properties (mass, radius, density, gravity), orbital dynamics 
  (semi-major axis, orbital velocity), and stellar radiation from its host star.

• Noisy & Corrupted Survey Data:
  Raw astronomical survey datasets contain significant observational noise, 
  synthetically injected corruptions (e.g., hidden string values like 'invalid', 
  'abc'), unphysical metadata ('random_code', 'junk_status', 'irrelevant_note'), 
  and missing values.

• Core Challenge:
  Traditional manual astronomical calculations for thermal and atmospheric 
  equilibrium across thousands of candidate planets are computationally slow and 
  error-prone when noise is present.

• The Objective:
  Build a robust Machine Learning pipeline that detects noise, extracts key 
  astrophysical drivers, and accurately predicts planetary environmental conditions 
  (specifically Mean Surface Temperature) across celestial bodies.

3. Robust Modeling & Validation Architecture:
   • 80/20 Train-Test split with fixed random seeds for reproducibility.
   • Rigorous evaluation on completely unseen planetary data using R² Score 
     and Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE).
